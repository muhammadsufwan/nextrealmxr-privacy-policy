Privacy Policy

We at Next Realm XR respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this Privacy Policy.


This Privacy Policy explains who we are, what information our apps use, how we process it, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.


We are a developer and publisher of virtual reality games and applications made available on the Meta Horizon Store, owned by Meta Platforms, Inc. This includes VR Boxing Rise to Glory.


This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through our website and our apps published on the Meta Horizon Store ("Games") (together "Services").

1Information We Collect

We only use the information our Games need to work. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your email address, phone number, payment details or precise location inside our Games.

Meta Horizon Platform Data

With the permissions granted to our app by Meta, our Games read your Meta user ID, your Meta display name (username) and your profile picture. See section 2 for details.

Game Progress Data

Our Games save your in-game progress, such as level, experience points, match results, win rate, win streaks, daily login rewards, missions and game settings (for example music and sound on/off).

Device and Motion Data

To play in virtual reality, our Games use headset position, controller position and hand tracking data in real time. This data is used only while you play to control the game, and it is not stored or sent anywhere. Our Games do not use the microphone or cameras.

Information You Send Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

2Meta Horizon Platform Data

When you start our Game, it signs you in automatically with your Meta account through the Meta Horizon Platform SDK. No separate sign-up or password is required. The data we receive from Meta is:

DataWhy we use it
User IDTo identify you as the signed-in player and keep your saved profile and game progress linked to your Meta account on your device.
Display name (username)To show your name on the main menu, profile screen, pre-fight versus screen and in-fight scoreboard.
Profile pictureTo show your picture on the main menu and profile screen. The picture is downloaded from Meta's servers and saved on your device so it does not need to be downloaded every time.
Entitlement statusTo confirm the Game was obtained from the Meta Horizon Store.

We use this data only to provide these features inside the Game. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we do not sell it. If this data is not available, the Game uses a local profile named "Player" instead.

Meta's own handling of your account data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

3How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the Game: showing your name and picture, saving your progress, stats and settings, and confirming your entitlement.
  • To answer you: if you email us, we use your email address to reply to your question or request.
  • To comply with legal obligations: where required by applicable laws, regulations or legal processes.

4Where Your Data Is Stored

The Meta Horizon Platform Data and game progress data described above are stored only locally on your Meta Quest headset. Our Games do not send this data to our own servers or to any third party server. We do not run our own servers that store player data.

Our Games connect to the internet only to talk to Meta's services (for sign-in and entitlement) and to download your profile picture from Meta.

5Disclosure Of Information

We do not sell, rent or share Meta Horizon Platform Data or other user information with third parties. We do not currently use any third-party service providers, data processors, analytics or advertising services that receive this data.

  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information we hold if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request. Since the data stays on your device, we usually hold no such data apart from emails you send us.
  • Changes: if we ever start using a service provider that would receive user data, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so.

6Data Retention

Data saved on your headset stays there until you delete the Game or clear its data. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer your request, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

7Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure or destruction. Data is kept on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. However, no security measures can guarantee absolute protection of user information.

8Children's Privacy

Our Games are intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9Data Deletion & Your Rights

You can delete all data saved by our Game at any time by uninstalling the Game from your Meta Quest headset, or by clearing the app's data in the headset settings. This removes your saved profile, cached profile picture, progress and settings.

You can also stop the Game from accessing your Meta account information by removing the app's permissions in your Meta account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. To make a request, or to ask us to delete any emails or other data we hold about you, email us at sufwan.saleem.meta@gmail.com. We will verify your request and delete the data unless we are required by law to keep it.

10Opt-Out

If you no longer wish to receive communications from us, you may opt out by emailing us at sufwan.saleem.meta@gmail.com.
